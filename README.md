INTERVIEW SKILLS
=====
Reflection
-----
**Situation:** I had just completed my interview skills test, and I was feeling a mix of excitement and nervousness because I wasn’t completely sure what to expect.

**Task:** My goal was to demonstrate my understanding of effective interview techniques, including body language, communication, and how to structure responses using the STAR method.

**Action:** During the test, I took my time to think through each question, applied the STAR approach where possible, and focused on being clear and confident in my answers.

**Result:** I realized I’ve improved a lot in how I express myself and structure my responses. The test helped me identify areas where I can still build more confidence and give stronger examples in future interviews.


***
MOCK INTERVIEW 
=====
Reflection
-----

**Situation:** It was my first time doing a mock interview, and I felt quite nervous because I had never experienced an interview setting before.

**Task:** My goal was to answer the questions clearly and professionally using the STAR technique to show my skills and experiences effectively.

**Action:** I tried to structure my responses by explaining the situation, what my role was, what actions I took, and the results of those actions. I focused on staying calm and thinking through each answer before speaking.

**Result:** Although I was a bit anxious, I managed to answer most questions well. I learned how helpful the STAR method is for keeping my answers organized and now feel more confident about improving for future real interviews.
